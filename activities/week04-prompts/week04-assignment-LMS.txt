3. RUBRIC (10 POINTS) | 評価基準（10点）
------------------------------------------------------------

Each row is scored Full, Partial, or Missing. This is a draft: assumptions do not lose points if they are clearly labeled.
各行を「満点／一部／なし」で評価します。草案なので、仮定は明確に印をつけていれば減点しません。

R1  Target user and context of use — 2 points
    Full (2): a specific, real user group (not "students" or "users"); context of use covers who (goals, abilities, limits), what (task), and where (physical, social, technical environment).
    Partial (1): group too broad, or one of who / what / where missing.
    Missing (0): no user group or context.

R2  Goals and frustrations — 2 points
    Full (2): 2–3 goals and 2–3 frustrations, each specific enough to settle a design decision (a concrete task, time, place, device, or number); no generic items like "wants a good experience."
    Partial (1): too few, or mostly generic.
    Missing (0): none.

R3  Summary and quote — 1 point
    Full (1): a name and one-line summary that capture the person; a quote in their own voice, labeled as a real person's words [Interview] or as invented [Assumption].
    Partial (0.5): summary generic, or quote unlabeled.
    Missing (0): neither.

R4  AI disposition — 2 points
    Full (2): trust level (too much / too little / about right) with a reason; a notably good or bad AI experience; a default interaction mode (direct manipulation, conversation, or delegation) with a reason linked to this interface.
    Partial (1): one or two of these, or no reason given.
    Missing (0): no AI disposition.

R5  Evidence notes — 2 points
    Full (2): every part labeled Interview, Observation, or Assumption; at least 2 key assumptions named, each with an open question to ask a real person before the next class.
    Partial (1): some parts unlabeled, or no assumptions to check.
    Missing (0): no evidence notes.

R6  AI transparency log — 1 point
    Full (1): tool and date; complete chats attached; at least 3 specific things you kept or changed from the AI's drafts, and why; how you checked the AI's output. (Or "No AI used.")
    Partial (0.5): chats missing, or changes vague.
    Missing (0): AI used but no log.

Deductions | 減点
- Real names or identifying details of real people in any file (including the chats): −1, and you will be asked to resubmit.
  いずれかのファイル（会話を含む）に実在の人の名前や個人が分かる情報がある場合：−1（再提出を求めます）。
- Assumptions presented as interviews or observations (for example, an invented quote labeled [Interview]): R5 is scored Partial or lower.
  仮定をインタビューや観察として示した場合（作った引用に [Interview] とつけるなど）：R5は「一部」以下。
- Persona submitted as AI output with no changes (log does not show changes, or the draft matches the chat word for word): R1–R4 are scored at most Partial.
  AIの出力をそのまま提出した場合（ログに変更点がない、または草案が会話と一字一句同じ）：R1〜R4は最大でも「一部」。

5. RULES FOR AI USE | AI利用のルール
------------------------------------------------------------

- AI can help you brainstorm and tighten your wording, but the person and the research judgment must be yours. Disclose all AI use in File 2. AI is a tool, not an author.
  AIはアイデア出しや言葉の調整に使えますが、人物像と調査上の判断はあなた自身のものであること。AIの利用はファイル2ですべて開示する。AIは道具であり、著者ではない。
- AI output can contain errors, invented facts, and stereotypes. Anything the AI suggested is an Assumption until a real person confirms it.
  AIの出力には誤り、作られた事実、ステレオタイプが含まれることがある。AIが出したことは、実在の人が確かめるまでは「仮定」。
- Practicing with the Rehearsal Agent does not count as checking an assumption. Only a real person can do that.
  リハーサル・エージェントとの練習は、仮定の確認にはならない。確かめられるのは実在の人だけ。
- Use only free stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels) or illustrations for the persona image. Never use a photo of a real person you know.
  ペルソナ画像は無料のストックフォト（Unsplash、Pexels）かイラストのみ。知っている実在の人の写真は使わない。
- Turn off "use my chats for training" in your AI tool if possible.
  可能なら、AIツールの「会話を学習に使う」設定をオフにする。

Before the next class: talk to one real person who resembles your persona, even briefly, and bring your notes. Next week you will complete the persona and add an accessibility audit.
