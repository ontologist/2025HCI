Week 4: Persona Draft (Part 1 of 2)

User-Centered Design, Applied to a Real Target User

第4週：ペルソナ草案（2回のうちの1回目）— ユーザー中心設計を実際のターゲットユーザーに適用する

📝 This is a draft deliverable. You will complete and validate this persona as part of the Week 5 assignment, alongside an accessibility audit. Don't aim for a polished final version this week.
これは草案の提出物です。第5週の課題で、アクセシビリティ監査とあわせてこのペルソナを完成・検証します。今週は完成版を目指さないでください。

📋 Assignment Overview / 課題概要

In this assignment, you'll draft a research-grounded persona for a real interface — your own project, or an interface you already use. Unlike a typical persona, yours must also include an AI disposition: how this person relates to AI assistants and agents.

この課題では、実際のインターフェースのための、調査に基づくペルソナ案を作成します。自分のプロジェクトでも、すでに使っているインターフェースでも構いません。典型的なペルソナと異なり、あなたのペルソナには、AIに対する態度、つまりこの人物がAIアシスタントやエージェントとどう関わるかも含める必要があります。

🎯 Learning Objectives / 学習目標

  • Apply ISO 9241-210's "understand context of use" activity to a specific user group
  • Build a persona from research or clearly flagged assumptions, not guesswork presented as fact
  • Write goals, frustrations, and context specific enough to use in a real design decision
  • Add an AI-disposition dimension connected to the three basic ways people interact with interfaces

📝 Activity Description / アクティビティ説明

Step 1: Pick a Real Target User Group (ターゲットユーザー層の選択)

  • Choose an interface you care about — a project idea, or something you already use
  • Pick one specific group of real people who would use it

Step 2: Draft the Core Persona (ペルソナの核)

  • Name and a one-line summary
  • 2–3 specific goals (not "wants a good experience")
  • 2–3 specific frustrations
  • Context of use: where, when, under what conditions they'd use this
  • One representative quote, in their own voice

Step 3: Add the AI Disposition (AIに対する態度)

  • How much does this person trust AI — too much, too little, or about right?
  • Have they had a notably good or bad experience with AI before?
  • Which of the three basic interaction modes (direct manipulation, conversation, delegation) would they default to, and why?

Step 4: Mark Your Evidence (根拠の明示)

  • For each part of the persona, note what it's based on: an interview, an observation, or an assumption you still need to check
  • It's fine — expected, even — to have assumptions at this stage. Flagging them clearly is the point

📦 Deliverables / 提出物

Persona Draft

Format: PDF document (1 page) or a single slide

Required Components:
  • Name and one-line summary
  • 2–3 goals and 2–3 frustrations
  • Context of use
  • One representative quote
  • AI disposition, connected to the three interaction modes (direct manipulation, conversation, delegation)
  • Evidence notes: what's research-based and what's still an assumption
  • AI transparency log (if used): tool, date, what you asked, what you kept or changed, how you checked it

✅ Submission Requirements / 提出要件

  • File Format: PDF (preferred)
  • File Naming: LastName_FirstName_Week04_Draft.pdf
  • Length: 1 page — this is a draft, not a full report
  • Work style: individual
  • Due Date: Before next class session
  • Note: this draft feeds directly into your Week 5 submission, which completes the persona and adds an accessibility audit

🤖 AI Use on This Assignment / この課題でのAI使用

An AI assistant can help you brainstorm goals and frustrations, or tighten your persona's quote — but the person and the research judgment behind them should be yours.

If you use one, log it: which tool, when, what you asked, what you kept or changed, and how you checked it.

💡 Tips for Success / 成功のためのヒント

  • Think of someone real: a specific person you know makes a far better starting point than an invented stereotype
  • Specific beats polished: a rough draft with one vivid, true detail beats a slick one with none
  • Flag your assumptions honestly: next week's assignment will ask you to go check them
  • Don't skip the AI disposition: it's easy to forget, and it matters for everything we design for the rest of the course
